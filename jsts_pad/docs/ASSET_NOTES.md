# JSTS Pad artwork

The official 0.0.1-exp1 identity is the user-approved logo created from the original hand-drawn JSTS monogram idea:

- near-square front-facing notebook;
- warm parchment/aged-scroll cover colour and texture;
- black left spiral and dark right elastic;
- thick crimson-red JSTS monogram, visually resembling a Japanese character/pagoda;
- monogram concept: an S over a J and an S over a T, with J/T united by the shared top bar;
- no product-name text on the icon;
- transparent outside background;
- no cast shadow, reflection, glow or halo.

`assets/icons/jsts-pad-master.png` is the 1024×1024 RGBA source master used to derive the hicolor PNG family. `jsts-pad.svg` is a self-contained scalable wrapper around that approved raster artwork; it does not redraw or reinterpret the monogram.
